Test build of "Prism", a puzzle game about guiding light and mixing colors.

What you can try in this build
- All 51 stages (10 free / 40 standard / 1 extra)
- Placing parts: drag and drop from the palette, tap to select then drag on the
  board for a placement preview, tap to rotate, drag to move, drag off the board
  to return a part to your inventory
- Light particle effects, sparkles on activated crystals, clear burst
- Clear marks on completed stages (kept across app launches)

What to focus on
- Does the board respond where your finger actually is (no offset in the preview)?
- Are the stages unreasonably hard, or too easy?
- Can you tell the light colors and strengths apart by eye (especially strong vs weak)?
- Any slowdown, particularly on the larger 9x9 and 10x10 boards?

Known limitations
- In-app purchases (Standard / Extra packs) are tested in the sandbox environment
- No music or sound effects yet
